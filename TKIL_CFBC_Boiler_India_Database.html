Unique TKIL Cold Cyclone — Derincoat Opportunity

🎯 Strategic Differentiator: TKIL's In-House Cold Cyclone Technology

  • TKIL is the ONLY Indian CFBC OEM with a fully in-house cold cyclone design — no licensor dependency. This means the cyclone geometry, dimensions, and thermal profiles are unique to TKIL.
  • Cold cyclone operating temperature: typically 150–350°C on the outer shell — ideal for Derincoat flexible blanket insulation systems.
  • Every TKIL CFBC plant in India has at least one cold cyclone separator. With 70+ boilers commissioned, this represents a massive installed base.
  • Derincoat flexible blanket systems can be marketed specifically as "TKIL Cold Cyclone Compatible" — a unique product positioning angle.
  • Cold cyclones typically have 2–4 metre diameter cylindrical bodies; insulation panels must accommodate curved geometry — conventional rigid systems perform poorly. Derincoat excels here.
  • DEMECH should propose a standardised "TKIL Cold Cyclone Insulation Kit" in partnership with TKIL's Service division.
